      \end{block}}
      \uncover<3->{
      \begin{block}{Hard-label student, seed 2 (116 tokens)}
        \scriptsize\ttfamily
        she eats 16 eggs * 3 = 48 eggs for breakfast. [...]\\

        The final answer is 232.
      \end{block}}
      \uncover<4->{
      \scriptsize The untrained student solves it step by step in 262 tokens: $16 - 7 = 9$ eggs,
      \$18. With hard labels the student copies the short GSM8K style (median answer
      under 100 tokens instead of 217) and reasons worse.}
    \end{column}

              6.9 of evaluation (34 to 60 minutes per run).
      \end{itemize}

  \vspace{0.25cm}
  \begin{itemize}\small
    \item All distilled students write longer answers than before training, and
          4 to 12\% of them hit the 512-token cap (1.7\% before).
    \item Hard labels taught GSM8K's short style (median under 100 tokens), yet
          5 to 12\% of answers still ran to the cap.
    \item A cut-off answer is almost always wrong, so the cap costs every arm some
          accuracy, on-policy the most.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}
